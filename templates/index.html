    About

    Choosing where to go shouldn't take longer than the trip. YOUR PEACE GUIDE compares your budget, season, interests, trip length and region with a curated set of Indian destinations, then ranks the places whose character fits you best.

    Recommendations use content-based filtering: your answers become a short profile that is compared with each destination's profile using TF-IDF and cosine similarity, combined with how well budget and duration fit.
